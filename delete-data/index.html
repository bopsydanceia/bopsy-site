Delete your data

Your photos and videos, right now

In Bopsy, open Settings and tap Delete my photos and videos, then confirm. Every photo and video you made is deleted from our servers. Your plan and coins are kept. Photos are also erased on their own 30 days after upload.

Your whole account

Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] from Bopsy (Settings > Help and support > Contact support) and ask us to delete your account. We delete your anonymous account, photos, videos, reports and notification tokens within 30 days.

What stays: your purchase history at Apple and RevenueCat, and our accounting records of coins, which contain no photo or video. To stop paying, cancel your subscription with Apple: deleting your data doesn't cancel it.
