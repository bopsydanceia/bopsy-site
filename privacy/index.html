Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Bopsy turns one photo into a short dance video. This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Bopsy is made by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS] (“we”), the controller of your data. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

In short. In the app, you don't create an account with your name or email; on the web, you sign in with your email only when you unlock a video. We use the photo you pick only to check it and make your video. Photos are erased 30 days after upload. Your photos and videos are never used for ads, never sold, and our providers do not use them to train AI models. You can delete your photos and videos at any time in the app.

1. What we collect

  • The photo you pick and the videos made from it, including the free preview if you get one (3 seconds in the app, 2 seconds on the web).
  • An anonymous account ID created when you first open the app, kept in your iPhone's Keychain. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Purchases: which plan or coin pack you bought, when it renews or ends, and your coin balance. In the app, Apple handles payment; on the web, RevenueCat and Stripe do (section 4). We never see your full card number.
  • On the web only: your email address, to sign in with a 6-digit code or a link and to find your purchases; a random identifier stored in your browser, and your IP address, used only to limit free previews to one per browser and per network.
  • A device check (Apple DeviceCheck) so each device gets at most one free preview. It doesn't identify you.
  • Notification token, only if you allow notifications, and your notification choices (video updates, new dances and offers).
  • Usage and diagnostics: which screens you use, app events such as “video started”, and crash reports. We don't keep your IP address.
  • Reports you send about a video, with your optional note.
  • Advertising measurement, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks: app events (install, purchases) and your device's advertising identifier, used to learn which ads brought people to Bopsy. Your photos and videos are never part of this.

3. AI processing

We send your photo only after you tick the box confirming that the photo shows you or that you have the person's permission. First, [MODERATION PROVIDER] checks it and refuses photos of children, nudity and violence. Then the photo and the dance reference video go to our AI video provider, [AI VIDEO PROVIDER], which uses the Kling model to make your video. Our providers process your photo only to give us this service and do not use it to train AI models. [TO CONFIRM IN WRITING WITH EACH PROVIDER]

4. Who helps us

These providers process data for us, under contracts that limit them to our instructions:

  • Supabase (United States): database, accounts (anonymous in the app, email on the web), private storage of photos and videos, server functions, sign-in emails.
  • RevenueCat (United States): subscriptions and coin packs, linked to your account ID; on the web, also the checkout and billing of web payments (RevenueCat Web Billing), with your email for receipts.
  • Stripe (United States and Ireland): processing of web payments (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) and fraud prevention. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us.
  • Apple: App Store payments, DeviceCheck, push notifications.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivery of notifications.
  • [AI VIDEO PROVIDER] and [MODERATION PROVIDER]: see section 3.
  • PostHog (usage, without IP) and Sentry (crash reports, without IP).
  • Meta and TikTok: ad measurement, only if you allow tracking.

We don't sell your personal information. We share app events with Meta and TikTok for ad measurement only if you allow tracking; you can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. How long we keep it

  • Photos: erased 30 days after upload.
  • Videos: until you delete them in Settings > Delete my photos and videos, or ask us.
  • Free previews: 7 days.
  • Reports: 12 months.
  • Coin and purchase records, without any photo or video: as long as accounting rules require.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your photos and videos in the app at any time, or your whole account by asking us: see Delete your data.
  • Turn notifications and ad tracking off in your iPhone's Settings, and news about dances and offers off in Bopsy's Settings.
  • You can ask us for access to your data, a copy, correction or deletion, and object to or restrict some uses. Write to [SUPPORT EMAIL]; we answer within 30 days.
  • In the European Union you can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL). In California you have the rights above under the CCPA, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

7. Children

Bopsy is rated 16+ and is not meant for children. We refuse photos that look like they show a child. If you think a child's data reached us, write to us and we'll delete it.

8. Transfers and security

Our providers mainly process data in the United States. Transfers from the European Union rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. Photos and videos are stored privately, encrypted in transit, and only your account can reach them.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
