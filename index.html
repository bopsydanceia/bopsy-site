Questions

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes once your video is unlocked. You can close the page: it waits for you in My videos.

How much is it?

A weekly or yearly plan, with a launch offer for new subscribers. You see the exact price before paying, and you can cancel anytime.

Which photo works best?

A sharp, well-lit photo of one adult, head to toe: you, or someone who said yes. It works with your dog or cat too. Photos of children, nudity or violence are always refused.

What happens to my photo?

It’s only used to make your video. Photos are erased 30 days after upload, never used for ads, never sold. You can delete your photos and videos whenever you want.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from My account. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
