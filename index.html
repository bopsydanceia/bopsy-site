Questions?

The short answers.

How long does it take?

About 2 minutes once you unlock your video. You can leave the page and come back, your video waits for you.

Which photos work best?

A sharp photo of one adult, one dog or one cat, with the whole body in the frame. Use a photo of yourself, of an adult who agreed, or of your own pet. Photos of children, nudity or violence are always refused.

What happens to my photo?

It's used only to make your video. Photos are erased 30 days after upload, never used for ads and never sold. You can delete your photos and videos anytime.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in the app under My account, Manage subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What if my video fails?

Its coins come back to your balance automatically. No ticket to open.
