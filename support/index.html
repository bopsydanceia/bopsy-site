How do coins work?

Each video costs the coins shown on its dance. Your plan's coins reset every week (every month on the yearly plan) and don't roll over. Coins you buy never expire.

My video failed. Do I get my coins back?

Yes, automatically. If a video can't be made in about 10 minutes, it stops and its coins come back to your balance. A finished video can't be refunded.

How long does a video take?

Usually a couple of minutes. You can close the app; we'll let you know when it's ready if you allowed notifications.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bopsy, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds of purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new iPhone or reinstalled Bopsy

Open the paywall or Settings in Bopsy and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription and the coins you bought come back. Your videos stay on the old device's account: save the ones you want to Photos before you switch.

A photo was refused

Use a sharp photo of one adult, dog or cat, with the whole body in the frame. Photos of children, nudity or violence are always refused. A refused photo costs no coins.

How do I report a video?

Tap the flag on the video, or go to Settings > Report a video. We look at every report.
